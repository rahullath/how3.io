Stability Score Explanation

How the stability score is calculated

The stability score measures how consistent a project's revenue is over time. Higher values indicate more consistent revenue with less volatility.

It's calculated by analyzing quarter-over-quarter revenue fluctuations, with lower volatility resulting in higher stability scores.

Magnitude Score Explanation

How the magnitude score is calculated

The magnitude score represents the relative size of a project's revenue. Higher values mean larger revenue compared to other projects.

It's calculated using a logarithmic scale to prevent very large projects from dominating the score.
